# TerraForge — Legal and Product Boundaries

**Not legal advice. Obtain qualified legal and tax advice before public launch or accepting money.**

## Product representation
- Parcels and collectibles are virtual game-world assets only, not real-world land deeds, title, property or investment interests.
- The current browser prototype uses editable localStorage. It does not establish enforceable ownership.
- The current collectible forge does not mint blockchain NFTs.
- The current marketplace does not process orders or transfer funds.
- The configured treasury address is a local setting, not a payment rail. No funds are routed to it.
- Do not advertise simulated balances, fees, yields or ownership as cash, guaranteed returns or real assets.

## Monetization launch gates
Before accepting funds, publish clear Terms, Privacy, fees, refund/cancellation, marketplace, risk and virtual-asset disclosures. Review applicable consumer protection, tax, payment, AML/sanctions, privacy and digital-asset rules with qualified counsel and payment providers.

## Security
Keep provider secrets and signing keys server-side. Verify payment webhooks, wallet signatures and chain confirmations. Do not rely on browser redirects, client-calculated prices or localStorage as proof of payment or ownership.
